Getting started

Setting up in ten minutes

Create your account with your business name. Open Requirements to set opening hours and open days, then add the shifts you run (for example "Morning 6am–12pm: 1 lead + 2 baristas"). Open Staff and add your people with their roles. Back on Schedule, click Generate Schedule.

Adding your first employees

On the Staff page click Add Employee. Give each person at least one role, their weekly minimum and maximum hours, and full-time or part-time. Tick Send portal invitation so they get a login and can manage their own availability.

What the three views mean

Table shows each person's shifts by day, best for a quick read. Grid shows the day as a visual column, great for small teams. Timeline shows every hour across the day, best for larger teams.

Generating schedules

Why are some hours still open?

Under the metrics you'll find Scheduler notes. Every open hour has a reason: nobody with that role is available, everyone is at their max hours or days, or a rule (minimum shift length, supervision) blocked it. Each reason comes with a suggestion.

Getting a different schedule

Click Find Alternative. The next result is forced to differ from the ones you've already seen while still following every rule. Reset clears that history.

Hard rules vs preferences

Availability, max hours, max shift length, split-shift limits, and supervision are always enforced. Minimum hours, rest between shifts, consecutive days, and preferred hours are strong preferences: the scheduler does its best and tells you when it couldn't.

How long should it take?

Most teams under 20 people finish in a few seconds. Fifty people with many roles can take 10–25 seconds. The progress view shows what it's doing; you can keep the tab open or come back.

Your staff's portal

Inviting staff and their logins

Invitations create a login with a temporary password that is emailed (and texted, if SMS is set up). On first sign-in they choose their own password. Managers can also open any employee's portal from the Staff page.

Availability and time off

Staff set their availability to the quarter hour on My Availability and request time off there. You approve or deny requests from the Staff page; approved days are blocked in future schedules and any already-scheduled shifts on those days are removed.

Shift swaps

From My Schedule, an employee taps a shift and chooses Request Swap. Only coworkers with the right role, free at that time, and under their hour limits are asked. When someone accepts, the published schedule updates itself and you get an email.

Notifications

Published schedules, swap requests, and time-off decisions go out by email and SMS. Each employee can turn either channel off in their portal menu.

Rules and coverage

Shifts vs. detailed coverage

Shifts mode is the simple one: name a shift, its hours, and how many of each role it needs. Detailed mode sets a default headcount per role for every hour, with peak-period boosts. Most businesses only need Shifts.

Minimum and maximum staff

Each role in a shift has a minimum (what the scheduler must fill) and a maximum (it will never schedule more). Set the maximum higher than the minimum if you're happy with extra hands when people need hours.

Max days per week: required or preferred?

"Required" is a hard cap. "Preferred" lets the scheduler go over when that's the only way to cover a shift, and it tells you when it did.

Supervision

Mark new hires as needs supervision and experienced staff as can supervise. A supervised person is only ever scheduled while a supervisor is also on the clock.

Troubleshooting

"No schedule possible with these rules"

This is rare and means the hard rules contradict each other, for example a minimum shift length longer than the max hours per day. Check the scheduler notes, relax one rule, and generate again.

A change didn't save

Every edit is saved to the server immediately and you'll see a confirmation toast. If you don't, check your connection and reload the page. Still stuck? Tell us what you changed.

I forgot my password

Use Forgot password on the sign-in page. The reset link arrives by email and works for one hour.

An employee can't see their schedule

Only published schedules appear in the portal. Generate, review, then click Publish on the schedule page.

Account and billing

Changing your plan

Plans can be changed or cancelled any time from account settings. Changes apply at the next billing cycle.

Deleting your account

Open Settings and choose Delete account. This removes your businesses, staff, and schedules permanently.
