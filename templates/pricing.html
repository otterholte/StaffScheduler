Questions people ask

How does the founding-members offer work?

Enter FOUNDER50 in the promo code box when you create your account. The first 50 businesses that do get every Professional feature free, for as long as you use the product. We ask in return that you tell us what's confusing or broken.

Do I need a credit card?

No. Starter is free forever and never asks for one, and founding members are never charged. Paid plans will ask for a card only when you choose to upgrade.

Can I change or cancel later?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your schedules and staff stay available on the free Starter plan. Delete your account from the settings page whenever you want and everything is removed.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Yes. Qualified nonprofits get 50% off any paid plan. Contact us to apply.
