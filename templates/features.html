Schedules in seconds, not evenings

A constraint solver weighs availability, roles, hour limits, rest between shifts, fairness, and preferences all at once. Most teams get a full-coverage schedule in under ten seconds, with a progress view while it works.

Explains what it couldn't fill

Open hours come with a reason ("both baristas are at max hours") and a suggestion, so you know exactly what to change.

Shift swaps that run themselves

Staff give away or trade shifts from their phone. Only eligible coworkers are asked, the schedule updates itself, and you get a note when it's done.

Availability and time off

Employees set their own availability to the quarter hour and request time off. Approved days are blocked automatically in the next schedule.

Email and text notifications

Published schedules, swap requests, approvals, and time-off decisions go out by email and SMS. Everyone chooses what they want to receive.

Rules you actually care about

Every rule is a plain setting, and each one can be a hard limit or a preference.

  • Minimum and maximum shift length
  • Max hours per day and per week
  • Max days per week for full- and part-time
  • Rest between a closing and an opening shift
  • Split shifts, per day and per week
  • New hires only work with a supervisor
  • Overtime allowed or avoided per person
  • Fair weekend rotation over time
